Before you enquire

Is my money safe with a chit fund?

It depends on choosing a registered, long-running company. Virchakra has run continuously in Basavanagudi since 1990.

How is this better than a bank RD?

You can bid for the pooled amount before maturity, and the effective borrowing cost is usually lower than market interest rates.

What if I need to withdraw partway?

Withdrawal is permitted; the company arranges a replacement subscriber and you are repaid at the end of the chit period.
