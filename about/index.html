About us

Registered, and run the same way since 1990.

We are a registered chit fund company catering to the financial needs of every class of people, in every walk of life.

Who we are

We connect people with the funds they need, when they need them.

We understand the necessity of financial independence, and we connect subscribers with the funds they require, whenever they require it and for whatever purpose. You can effortlessly save, or borrow from us, by choosing from a range of chit groups sized to different budgets.

Chit funds carry a reputation problem: people ask whether the plans are legitimate, whether there are legal safeguards, and whether this is simply another money-multiplier scheme in disguise. Those concerns are fair, because unorganised operators have cheated subscribers elsewhere. We are a registered chit fund company, and our schemes, work modules and payouts follow the same systematic process for every group we run.
