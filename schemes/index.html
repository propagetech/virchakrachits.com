Doorstep collection

Instalments are collected at the subscriber's doorstep, free of cost, by a professional from our branch, with a proper receipt every time.

Choose your term

Most chit values are offered at more than one duration, so you can trade a lower instalment for a longer term.

Bid when you need it

Any non-prized subscriber who is current on instalments can bid in the monthly auction for the pooled amount.
